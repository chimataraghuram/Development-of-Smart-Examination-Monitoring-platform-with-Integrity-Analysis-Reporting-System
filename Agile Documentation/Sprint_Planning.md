# Sprint Planning

**Cadence:** 2-week sprints
**Team:** Smart Examination Monitoring Team
**Definition of Ready:** Story has a user, value, and acceptance criteria.

## Sprint 1 — Foundation

**Goal:** Candidates and admins can authenticate; SQLite schema exists.
**Planned:** US-01, US-02, initial database, login/register UI.

## Sprint 2 — Monitoring core

**Goal:** Face and browser events are detected and stored with evidence.
**Planned:** US-03 (partial), US-05, US-06; OpenCV cascade; event logging.

## Sprint 3 — Exam lifecycle and scoring

**Goal:** Sessions can be started, paused, resumed, and ended with an integrity score.
**Planned:** US-03 (complete), scoring engine, examination CRUD (US-07).

## Sprint 4 — Review, reporting, and assistant

**Goal:** Admins can review candidates, export reports, and use the AI assistant.
**Planned:** US-04, US-08, US-09, export service, dashboard analytics polish.
